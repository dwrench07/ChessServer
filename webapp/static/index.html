Every finished game earns XP. As you level up, your realm grows from a lone camp to a dynasty. A well-played loss still builds more than a sloppy win — chess rewards understanding, not just results.

The campaign

A ladder of opponents, each a character with their own style. Level up to unlock the next challenger; beat one (win a game at their strength or higher) to claim the rung. Standard chess throughout — only the personas change.
